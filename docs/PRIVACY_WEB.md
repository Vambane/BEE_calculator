## What you enter

Company-level figures you type in to score a B-BBEE scorecard (for example turnover, ownership and management percentages and spend figures). If you use the procurement calculator, the supplier list you upload or type in: supplier names, spend, B-BBEE level, size and ownership percentages. If you use the contributions calculator, the contribution lines you type in: beneficiary names, contribution types and amounts. If you use the Net Value calculator, share values, acquisition debt and dates.

## Why

To calculate an indicative B-BBEE score and let you print or save a gap report of that calculation. Race- and gender-linked aggregates are processed only for that scorecard purpose (POPIA s26).

## Consent

You must tick that you have read this notice and agree to this processing for this scorecard before scoring proceeds.

## Where your figures are processed

The calculator runs entirely in your web browser. Your figures are processed on your own device and are **not sent to us or to any server**. When you close or reload the page, they are cleared from the page.

| Data | Where it lives | How long | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scorecard inputs (turnover, ownership %, etc.) | Your browser's memory, on your device | Until you close or reload the page, or choose Start over | Never sent to a server |
| Supplier list (procurement calculator) | Your browser's memory, on your device | Until you close or reload the page, or choose Start over | Download it as CSV to keep it |
| Net Value inputs (share values, debt, dates) | Your browser's memory, on your device | Until you close or reload the page, or choose Start over | Never sent to a server |
| Contribution lines (benefit-factor calculator) | Your browser's memory, on your device | Until you close or reload the page, or choose Start over | Never sent to a server |
| Client file (save / open) | A file you download to your own device; read in your browser when you open it | As long as you keep the file | We never receive a copy; consent is not stored in the file |
| Gap report | Built in your browser; you print it or save it as PDF | As long as you keep the file | We never receive a copy |

## The website itself

The site's files (the page, the calculator engine and fonts) are served by our hosting provider, Cloudflare. Like any website, delivering those files involves standard request data such as your IP address and browser type, which the hosting provider processes to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. None of your calculator figures are part of these requests. We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.

## Email and marketing

The browser version does not ask for your email address.

## Your rights

Your figures never leave your device, so there is nothing for us to export or delete. Close the page or choose Start over to clear them.

## Hosting and cross-border processing

The site's files are delivered through Cloudflare's global network, so request data for delivering the files may be processed outside South Africa. A POPIA s72 basis for that transfer is to be recorded (legal to confirm the exact ground). Your calculator figures are not transferred anywhere.

## Changes

We will update this notice before the browser version collects any personal information beyond what is described here.
